## 4. TradingView Drawing Engine & Customizer (`@drawing-ux` Gap Analysis vs Local Code)

### Concrete Weaknesses Pinpointed in Current Local `DrawingOverlay.tsx` & `DrawingSettingsModal.tsx`

1. **No Pre-Draw Floating Toolbar (`ev_d2fd4f3b03c2d6eef5e7ab607613fe437e620d77658a18eb6c47494cabae5761`)**:
   - In `DrawingOverlay.tsx:2457`, `{selectedDrawing && !hideDrawings && (<div data-testid="drawing-floating-toolbar">...)}` only mounts *after* a drawing is placed and selected!
   - When a trader selects `Rectangle` or `Trendline` (`activeTool !== 'crosshair'`), there is **no floating toolbar** to pick dotted/dashed/solid, border color, fill color, or width before drawing, and active drafts (`DrawingOverlay.tsx:2413`) hardcode `'#2962ff'` with forced `'4 4'` dash instead of previewing `defaultToolStyles[activeTool]`!
2. **Border Stroke Coupled to Fill / Main Color (`DrawingOverlay.tsx:1161-1170`, `shared/types.ts:231-264`)**:
   - `rect`, `channel`, `gann_box`, `circle`, `triangle`, `callout`, `text`, `measure`, and `price_range` lack independent `borderColor`, `borderWidth` (`0` = borderless shaded zone!), `borderStyle` (`'solid' | 'dashed' | 'dotted'`), and `showBorder`!
   - Shipped TradingView bundle `76614.af9cdba9b5607964c040.js` explicitly separates `outlineBorder?.width` & `outlineBorder?.color` from background fill and text color (`en.32879`: `"Border"`, `"Background"`, `"Border and fill colors"`).
   - Also in `workspaceStore.ts:1207-1224` (`addDrawing`), `saveDrawingTemplate`, and `applyDrawingTemplate`, new border/text fields (`borderColor`, `borderWidth`, `borderStyle`, `showBorder`, `textColor`, `textBold`, `textItalic`) must be merged and persisted into `defaultToolStyles` and `drawingTemplates`!
3. **Only 2 Corner Handles on Rectangles Instead of 8-Point Resize Handles (`DrawingOverlay.tsx:864-891`)**:
   - Currently `wrapGroup` only maps `px.map((pt, idx) => <circle r={5} fill="#131722" ... />)` — giving a rectangle only **2 opposite corner dots** (`points[0]` and `points[1]`)! You cannot drag the top edge (`N`), bottom edge (`S`), left/right edges (`W`/`E`), or the other two corners (`NE`/`SW`)!
   - **TradingView Parity Fix**:
     - For 2-point bounding-box tools (`rect`, `gann_box`, `measure`, `price_range`, `date_range`, `anchored_vp`), compute `minTime/maxTime` & `maxPrice/minPrice` (or pixel `left/right/top/bottom`) and render **8 white-filled, blue-ringed TradingView handles** (`r=5.5`, `fill="#ffffff"`, `stroke="#2962ff"`, `strokeWidth=2`):
       - 4 corners: `NW` (`nwse-resize`), `NE` (`nesw-resize`), `SE` (`nwse-resize`), `SW` (`nesw-resize`)
       - 4 edge midpoints: `N` (`ns-resize`, adjusts top price only), `S` (`ns-resize`, adjusts bottom price only), `W` (`ew-resize`, adjusts start time only), `E` (`ew-resize`, adjusts end time only).
     - Use white-filled `#ffffff` + `#2962ff` stroke handles on all other tools too so handles pop crisply on both dark `#131722` and white `#ffffff` ICT charts!
4. **Missing / Thin Hit-Testing Corridors on Shapes, Channels, Fibs & Pitchforks (`DrawingOverlay.tsx:1161-1614`)**:
   - TradingView's `LineRenderer.hitTest` (`76614.af9cdba9b5607964c040.js`) computes `(hitTestTolerance ?? interactionTolerance().line) + linewidth / 2` across extended segments (`extendAndClipLineSegment`).
   - In our `DrawingOverlay.tsx`, `channel` lines (`L1466-1478`), `fib` / `fib_ext` level lines (`L1380-1388`), `fib_fan` rays (`L1288-1295`), `pitchfork` tines (`L1587-1612`), `crossline` (`L923-924`), `rect` border when `showFill === false` (`L1161`), `circle` (`L2058`), and `triangle` (`L2066`) have **NO transparent `strokeWidth={16}` hit-corridor**, making thin `1px` or `dotted` lines/borders frustrating to click! Add `stroke="transparent" strokeWidth={16}` hit lines/rects/paths on all of them (and respect `lineStyle` on `vline`, `crossline`, `extended`, `arrow`, `callout`, `rect`, `circle`, `triangle`, `gann_box`!).
5. **Cramped 6-Dot Floating Toolbar & Modal Without Cancel/Template Footer**:
   - Upgrade `drawing-floating-toolbar` to show in **both Pre-Draw (`activeTool !== 'crosshair'`) and Selected (`selectedDrawing !== null`) modes**, with rich TradingView popovers (`drawing-color-popover-btn`, `drawing-fill-popover-btn`, `drawing-border-popover-btn`), crisp SVG line-style buttons (`drawing-style-solid` Normal, `drawing-style-dashed` Dashed, `drawing-style-dotted` Dotted), width `1px–4px` (`drawing-width-1`..`4`), while keeping all existing `data-testid`s intact.
   - Upgrade `DrawingSettingsModal.tsx` with explicit **Line / Stroke**, **Border** (`[✓] Border`, color swatch grid, `1–5px` width, `Normal / Dashed / Dotted`), **Background** (`[✓] Background`, color swatch grid, `0%–90%` opacity), and **Text** (`Text Color`, `Font Size`, `Bold`, `Italic`, `Alignment`, `Text Box Border`) sections, plus pre-open snapshot + **`Template ⌄`** | **`Cancel`** (reverts!) | **`Ok`** (`data-testid="drawing-settings-done-btn"`).
